Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what information Shri Arjuna collects when you book a consultation and how it is used.

What we collect

Your name, mobile number, email address, chosen service and time, and the answers you give in the booking form (for example birth details for astrology, or the location of a meeting). Payment is processed by Razorpay; we do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

How we use it

Only to schedule and conduct your consultation, send calendar invites and reminders, and contact you about your booking.

Confidentiality

What you discuss in a consultation is kept strictly confidential and is never shared with anyone else. We do not sell or rent your information.

Service providers

Bookings are managed with Cal ID and Google Calendar, payments with Razorpay, and messages may be sent through WhatsApp. These providers process data only to deliver these services.

Retention and your choices

We keep booking records only as long as needed for the consultation and for accounting. You may ask us to correct or delete your information by contacting us.
