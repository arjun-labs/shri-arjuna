Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Payment is made in advance to confirm a consultation. This policy explains what happens if a booking is changed or cancelled.

If we cannot accept your booking

If the requested time cannot be accepted, we will offer you another time. If you do not want the new time, you will receive a full refund.

If you cancel

If you cancel at least 24 hours before the consultation, you will receive a full refund. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the consultation, or not attending, are generally not refundable, because the time has already been reserved for you. Any exception is at our discretion.

Additional time

Fees for additional sessions (beyond the booked session time) are for time already used and are not refundable.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through Razorpay. Banks usually take 5–7 working days to show the amount in your account.
